# Barni Customer Journey

## Purpose

This document describes the complete experience of a restaurant owner using Barni—from the first minute to the point where Barni becomes a dependable part of daily operations.

The journey is not about teaching someone to use invoice software. It is about helping a busy restaurant owner build a Business Memory that saves time, saves money, reduces stress, and improves confidence.

Every stage should answer one primary question within five seconds. Barni should remain calm, clear, honest about uncertainty, and grounded in the restaurant's approved data.

# Stage 1 — First Launch

## Primary question

**What is Barni, and how will it help my restaurant?**

## What does the user see?

- A calm welcome from Barni.
- One short explanation: Barni learns from the restaurant's invoices and builds a useful Business Memory.
- One obvious primary action: **Feed Barni**.
- A quiet empty-state preview of what Barni will eventually remember: invoices, suppliers, products, and prices.
- No developer tools, database language, model configuration, fake metrics, or sample business conclusions.

## What should the user feel?

- Calm rather than overwhelmed.
- Curious about what Barni can learn.
- Safe giving Barni the first invoice.
- Confident that the product is designed for restaurant operations, not technical users.

## What should the user understand?

- Barni is an AI Business Memory and Operations Assistant.
- Barni is not accounting software and does not replace the accountant.
- Every approved invoice teaches Barni something about the business.
- Barni will not pretend to know anything before it has enough stored evidence.
- The restaurant remains in control of what enters Business Memory.

## User goal

Understand the value of Barni quickly and know exactly how to begin.

## Barni goal

Earn permission for the first useful action without demanding setup, training, or technical decisions.

## Potential friction

- The user may assume Barni is only an invoice scanner.
- An empty dashboard can feel unfinished.
- Product language such as “Business Memory” may be unfamiliar.
- Too many navigation choices can distract from the first upload.
- A long onboarding flow can feel like work before value.

## How Barni reduces stress

- Presents one sentence and one primary action.
- Explains empty states as the beginning of learning, not as missing configuration.
- Avoids technical terminology and setup choices.
- Makes it clear that nothing is saved as approved knowledge without review.

## How Barni builds trust

- Makes the limits of current knowledge visible.
- Explains how invoices become approved Business Memory.
- Does not show invented sample insights as restaurant facts.
- Uses calm, consistent language and design from the first screen onward.

## How Barni saves time

- Gets the user to the first upload immediately.
- Avoids tutorials that explain features before the user needs them.
- Uses sensible defaults for technical configuration.

# Stage 2 — First Upload

## Primary question

**How do I teach Barni something new?**

## User goal

Upload an invoice quickly and know that Barni is reading it correctly.

## Barni goal

Turn the document into a structured candidate, expose uncertainty, and keep the user in control before anything becomes approved memory.

## Ideal experience

1. The user drags in a PDF or image.
2. Barni confirms the upload.
3. Barni shows clear reading and preparation progress.
4. The invoice enters Review Queue.
5. Barni shows the extracted supplier, products, totals, confidence that genuinely exists, and specific fields needing attention.
6. The user corrects details and approves, rejects, skips, replaces, or keeps both when a duplicate is detected.
7. Barni confirms exactly what was learned.

## Potential friction

- Slow OCR can look frozen.
- Low-quality images can produce missing or incorrect fields.
- Confidence percentages can be misunderstood.
- Raw validation codes can feel technical.
- Duplicate choices can feel risky.
- A user may not understand the difference between upload, review, and approval.

## How Barni reduces stress

- Uses short, professional progress states: uploading, reading, checking, saving, and learning.
- Highlights only fields supported by real validation issues.
- Explains unavailable confidence instead of inventing it.
- Keeps failed or uncertain documents out of approved Business Memory.
- Explains the effect of Replace, Skip, and Keep both before the decision.

## How Barni builds trust

- Keeps the original document available beside extracted fields.
- Shows why an invoice needs review.
- Preserves the user's corrections and approval decision.
- Never silently discards a duplicate upload.
- Confirms the invoices, suppliers, products, and price points actually added.

## How Barni saves time

- Supports drag-and-drop and multiple files.
- Prepares fields and line items for review rather than manual entry.
- Directs attention only to uncertain or invalid details.
- Detects likely duplicates before they create cleanup work.

# Stage 3 — First Insight

## Primary question

**What has Barni learned that I did not immediately notice?**

## User goal

Receive the first useful observation from stored business data.

## Barni goal

Demonstrate that Business Memory creates value beyond document storage while remaining honest about limited history.

## Ideal experience

- After one approved invoice, Barni shows direct facts: the supplier, products, date, quantities, and prices learned.
- Barni does not call a first purchase an increase, decrease, stable price, or trend.
- After a second comparable purchase, Barni explains the first supported price movement in one sentence.
- The user can trace the observation to both source purchases.

## Potential friction

- The user may expect meaningful trends after one invoice.
- Similar product names or incompatible units can weaken comparisons.
- Too many metrics can hide the first useful observation.
- A percentage without reference values can be difficult to trust.

## How Barni reduces stress

- Says, “I need another purchase before I can calculate a trend” when history is insufficient.
- Leads with one clear observation rather than a dense analytics table.
- Shows the latest and previous values together.
- Surfaces ambiguous products or units instead of forcing a conclusion.

## How Barni builds trust

- Distinguishes facts from trends.
- Uses real previous purchases and approved product lines only.
- Excludes totals, VAT, payments, discounts, and notes from product analytics.
- Provides the supplier, product, dates, prices, and source invoices behind the insight.

## How Barni saves time

- Finds relevant changes automatically.
- Removes the need to compare invoices manually.
- Makes purchase history available from the product or supplier context.

# Stage 4 — First Recommendation

## Primary question

**Is there anything worth doing?**

## User goal

Understand whether a supported change deserves action and what a sensible next step could be.

## Barni goal

Turn reliable evidence into one conservative, actionable suggestion without overstating certainty.

## Ideal experience

- Barni first explains the fact or trend.
- The recommendation shows the product, current price, previous price, monetary or percentage difference, and source context.
- Barni suggests one proportionate action: monitor, review with the supplier, compare actual quotes, or take no action.
- The user chooses whether to act. Barni does not execute consequential actions automatically.

## Potential friction

- Recommendations can sound generic or alarmist.
- A user may confuse a suggestion with proof that another supplier is cheaper.
- Ordinary price variation can create unnecessary urgency.
- Too many recommendations can become noise.

## How Barni reduces stress

- Limits recommendations to the most useful supported actions.
- Uses calm language such as “Consider reviewing” rather than commands.
- Clearly shows when no action is recommended.
- Keeps facts, trends, and recommendations visually distinct.

## How Barni builds trust

- Explains before recommending.
- Never claims another supplier is cheaper without comparable stored evidence.
- Shows the rule and values that triggered the recommendation.
- Acknowledges missing history, unclear units, and uncertain product identity.

## How Barni saves time

- Prioritizes the changes most worth reviewing.
- Prepares evidence for a supplier conversation.
- Reduces time spent deciding whether a price movement is meaningful.

# Stage 5 — Daily Usage

## Primary question

**What needs my attention today?**

## User goal

Keep Business Memory current and understand the day's meaningful changes without managing another complicated system.

## Barni goal

Become a quiet operational habit: learn new documents, surface exceptions, and make routine information instantly available.

## Ideal experience

1. The owner opens Home and understands the current business state within five seconds.
2. Barni shows up to three useful, supported items that deserve attention.
3. The owner uploads new invoices or clears Review Queue.
4. Search answers immediate invoice, supplier, product, spending, or date questions.
5. Business Memory confirms what was learned.

## Potential friction

- Too many alerts or metrics can create alert fatigue.
- Review Queue can grow if uncertainty is not easy to resolve.
- Repeated technical choices can make daily use feel administrative.
- Slow pages can interrupt the restaurant's working rhythm.
- Navigation ambiguity can add unnecessary clicks.

## How Barni reduces stress

- Keeps Home focused on one current status and a few attention items.
- Uses calm empty states when nothing requires action.
- Separates primary actions from detailed history.
- Shows professional loading and completion states.
- Keeps developer and maintenance tools outside the restaurant workflow.

## How Barni builds trust

- Uses the same calculations across Home, Knowledge, Search, and recommendations.
- Keeps every important message traceable to approved records.
- Shows uncertainty consistently.
- Logs important failures without exposing technical details to the user.

## How Barni saves time

- Makes Feed Barni the single path for new documents.
- Prioritizes uncertain invoices rather than requiring review of everything equally.
- Provides immediate search across invoices, suppliers, products, and dates.
- Remembers previous prices and corrections so the user does not have to.

# Stage 6 — Weekly Review

## Primary question

**What changed this week, and what should I prepare for?**

## User goal

Review purchasing health, supplier movements, and unresolved issues before they become operational problems.

## Barni goal

Turn the week's approved activity into a concise, evidence-based operating brief.

## Ideal experience

- Barni summarizes approved invoices and spend for the period.
- Supplier and product changes are grouped by significance.
- Favorable movement, stable comparable prices, missing history, and negative movement remain distinct.
- Recommendations include source evidence and one suggested next step.
- Outstanding Review Queue items are visible before the review is considered complete.

## Potential friction

- Weekly totals can be distorted by missing or unapproved documents.
- Product descriptions and units may not be comparable.
- Repeating daily alerts adds noise instead of perspective.
- Dense tables can obscure the few decisions that matter.

## How Barni reduces stress

- Leads with a short weekly summary and the top supported changes.
- Separates incomplete data from genuine stability.
- Uses detailed tables only after the summary.
- Shows a calm “No action recommended” state when appropriate.

## How Barni builds trust

- States the date range and approved-data coverage.
- Links each important change to source invoices and purchases.
- Avoids supplier comparisons without compatible product and unit evidence.
- Preserves the calculation basis for savings or extra cost.

## How Barni saves time

- Replaces manual invoice-by-invoice comparison.
- Prepares supplier discussion points.
- Consolidates scattered product and spend history into one review.

# Stage 7 — Monthly Closing

## Primary question

**Is this month's business memory complete and ready to share?**

## User goal

Confirm the month's records, resolve outstanding issues, and prepare an accurate package for review or the accountant.

## Barni goal

Provide a safe, auditable closing workflow without pretending to replace accounting judgment.

## Ideal experience

1. Barni shows the month, approved document count, total, unresolved review items, and data-quality notices.
2. The owner resolves uncertain and duplicate documents.
3. Barni explains what the monthly package includes and excludes.
4. The owner downloads the package and explicitly closes the month.
5. Barni records the closing decision, date, and note.

## Potential friction

- Closing a month can feel irreversible or legally authoritative.
- Missing invoices may not be obvious.
- Technical export formats can be confusing.
- A default month can accidentally point to the wrong period.
- The owner may assume Barni validated tax compliance.

## How Barni reduces stress

- Uses a checklist before the closing action.
- Defaults to the relevant month but requires explicit confirmation.
- Explains the contents of the export package in plain language.
- Separates operational completeness from accounting or tax approval.
- Provides a clear closed/open status and recovery guidance.

## How Barni builds trust

- Uses only approved stored records.
- Shows unresolved items before closing.
- Preserves source documents, totals, timestamps, and the user's closing note.
- Makes the scope and limitations of Barni's validation explicit.
- Keeps the exported package reproducible and traceable.

## How Barni saves time

- Collects source documents and summaries into one package.
- Reduces manual file gathering and reconciliation preparation.
- Preserves the closing history for future reference.

# What Makes Users Come Back Every Morning?

Users come back when Barni makes the restaurant feel easier to run.

They return because Barni remembers what they would otherwise need to search for, compare manually, or keep in their head. It shows what changed, explains why it matters, and stays quiet when there is nothing useful to say.

The morning habit should be simple:

1. Open Barni.
2. Understand the state of the business within five seconds.
3. See whether anything deserves attention.
4. Teach Barni what is new.
5. Continue the day with greater confidence.

Barni earns that habit through trust, not engagement tricks. Every return visit should save time, reduce uncertainty, or support a better decision. When the restaurant owner feels that Barni understands the business, remembers reliably, and quietly helps before problems become expensive, Barni becomes part of the operation rather than another piece of software to maintain.
