# Barni MVP Roadmap

This roadmap translates the Barni Constitution, Product Blueprint, User Journeys, Personality, and Business Brain into a prioritized sequence of product outcomes. It deliberately avoids implementation details.

## Prioritization Standard

- **P0 — Critical for MVP:** Required for a trustworthy, complete core experience.
- **P1 — Important after MVP:** Deepens usefulness once the core loop is reliable.
- **P2 — Nice to have:** Improves retention or breadth without defining MVP readiness.
- **P3 — Future vision:** Extends Barni toward a more complete business companion after sufficient knowledge and trust exist.

Tasks are ordered globally. A later task may begin only when its listed dependencies are sufficiently trustworthy.

# P0 — Critical for MVP

## 1. Establish one truthful operational state

- **Why it matters:** Pending, reviewed, approved, duplicate, and ready states must mean the same thing everywhere. Contradictions immediately damage trust and make every later insight less credible.
- **Constitution principle supported:** Trust before capability; one canonical source of truth; no contradictory states.
- **Estimated effort:** Medium
- **Expected product impact:** High
- **Dependencies:** Clear product definitions for each operational state.
- **Recommended implementation order:** 1

## 2. Complete the Feed-to-Memory loop

- **Why it matters:** The defining MVP promise is not document upload. It is that Barni learns, asks only for necessary help, confirms completion, and grows Business Memory.
- **Constitution principle supported:** The core loop; Feed must finish the job; action before complexity.
- **Estimated effort:** Large
- **Expected product impact:** High
- **Dependencies:** Task 1; agreed criteria for when an invoice is reviewed and learned.
- **Recommended implementation order:** 2

## 3. Make uncertainty and required attention unambiguous

- **Why it matters:** Users need to know what requires judgment, why it requires attention, and what will happen after they respond. This is essential to completing daily work in under three minutes.
- **Constitution principle supported:** Trust before capability; Barni is calm; every detail view explains itself.
- **Estimated effort:** Medium
- **Expected product impact:** High
- **Dependencies:** Tasks 1–2; real uncertainty evidence.
- **Recommended implementation order:** 3

## 4. Deliver a truthful completion moment

- **Why it matters:** The daily journey needs a clear end. Users should leave knowing that nothing remains unresolved and what Barni genuinely learned.
- **Constitution principle supported:** Feed must finish the job; Barni evolves through real knowledge; silence is a feature.
- **Estimated effort:** Small
- **Expected product impact:** High
- **Dependencies:** Tasks 1–3; a supported summary of newly learned knowledge.
- **Recommended implementation order:** 4

## 5. Align Home around what matters now

- **Why it matters:** Home must offer one reliable answer to “What should I know or do right now?” and lead directly into the most useful next action.
- **Constitution principle supported:** Home is today; one screen, one job; meaning before data.
- **Estimated effort:** Medium
- **Expected product impact:** High
- **Dependencies:** Tasks 1–4; trustworthy attention and completion states.
- **Recommended implementation order:** 5

## 6. Make monthly accountant readiness trustworthy

- **Why it matters:** Owners need confidence that the monthly package is complete before approval or sending. Readiness must reflect the same review truth used throughout Barni.
- **Constitution principle supported:** Trust before capability; Accountant prepares clean, trustworthy output; action before complexity.
- **Estimated effort:** Medium
- **Expected product impact:** High
- **Dependencies:** Tasks 1–3; agreed monthly readiness criteria.
- **Recommended implementation order:** 6

## 7. Simplify customer navigation and remove unfinished surfaces

- **Why it matters:** Overlapping, empty, legacy, or internal destinations make the product feel like a collection of tools rather than one calm companion.
- **Constitution principle supported:** One screen, one job; customer product and internal tools are separate; no unfinished surfaces.
- **Estimated effort:** Medium
- **Expected product impact:** High
- **Dependencies:** Clear ownership for Home, Feed, Search, Business Memory, Insights, and Accountant; approval before removing or merging customer-facing destinations.
- **Recommended implementation order:** 7

## 8. Consolidate business knowledge into Business Memory

- **Why it matters:** Suppliers, products, prices, relationships, and history need one understandable home. Parallel knowledge destinations force users to learn the product's structure instead of learning about their business.
- **Constitution principle supported:** Business Memory is the knowledge model; one screen, one job; invoices are only one source of knowledge.
- **Estimated effort:** Large
- **Expected product impact:** High
- **Dependencies:** Task 7; trustworthy supplier and product identity; retained source evidence.
- **Recommended implementation order:** 8

## 9. Validate every MVP journey with real business data

- **Why it matters:** MVP quality depends on complete flows, not isolated screens. First Launch, Daily Use, Search, Business Memory, Insights, and Accountant must remain understandable across normal, empty, uncertain, and narrow-screen states.
- **Constitution principle supported:** Product quality gate; every screen must reduce effort; evidence before insight.
- **Estimated effort:** Medium
- **Expected product impact:** High
- **Dependencies:** Tasks 1–8; representative Hebrew and English business data.
- **Recommended implementation order:** 9

# P1 — Important after MVP

## 10. Turn Insights into an answer-first decision experience

- **Why it matters:** Users should see the most meaningful supported change, why it matters, its evidence, and one proportionate action before seeing charts or history.
- **Constitution principle supported:** Insights is meaning; meaning before data; evidence before insight.
- **Estimated effort:** Medium
- **Expected product impact:** High
- **Dependencies:** Reliable price and purchase history; stable supplier and product identity; completed MVP trust work.
- **Recommended implementation order:** 10

## 11. Make source evidence available wherever Barni makes a claim

- **Why it matters:** Quiet confidence becomes trust when users can inspect the invoice, purchase, or history behind a meaningful statement without being overwhelmed by technical detail.
- **Constitution principle supported:** Evidence before insight; every detail view explains itself; progressive disclosure.
- **Estimated effort:** Medium
- **Expected product impact:** High
- **Dependencies:** Task 10; reliable links between knowledge and source records.
- **Recommended implementation order:** 11

## 12. Strengthen supplier and product identity

- **Why it matters:** Aliases, packaging, units, and similar names must resolve conservatively. Price comparisons and purchasing patterns are only useful when Barni knows it is comparing the same real thing.
- **Constitution principle supported:** Trust before capability; Business Memory is the knowledge model; preserve uncertainty.
- **Estimated effort:** Large
- **Expected product impact:** High
- **Dependencies:** Task 8; sufficient historical evidence; user confirmation for uncertain relationships.
- **Recommended implementation order:** 12

## 13. Expand Search from finding records to answering remembered questions

- **Why it matters:** Search should increasingly feel like asking the business brain—returning concise, evidence-backed answers about suppliers, products, dates, prices, and review needs without becoming an archive.
- **Constitution principle supported:** Search is memory, not archive; evidence before insight; premium means less.
- **Estimated effort:** Large
- **Expected product impact:** High
- **Dependencies:** Tasks 8, 11, and 12; reliable underlying knowledge for each supported question.
- **Recommended implementation order:** 13

## 14. Establish a deliberate Hebrew and English experience

- **Why it matters:** Mixed-language business data is normal, but interface language and Barni's responses must remain intentional and predictable.
- **Constitution principle supported:** Language consistency; Barni is calm; no technical language in customer UI.
- **Estimated effort:** Medium
- **Expected product impact:** Medium
- **Dependencies:** Stable customer journeys and approved product vocabulary.
- **Recommended implementation order:** 14

## 15. Improve perceived speed across core journeys

- **Why it matters:** Barni should feel immediate during daily intake, Search, detail review, and Business Memory exploration. Waiting weakens the sense that Barni already knows the business.
- **Constitution principle supported:** Calm, premium, fast; product quality gate; every screen must reduce effort.
- **Estimated effort:** Medium
- **Expected product impact:** High
- **Dependencies:** Stable P0 experiences and representative production-scale data.
- **Recommended implementation order:** 15

# P2 — Nice to Have

## 16. Add recurring-purchase and missing-pattern intelligence

- **Why it matters:** Barni can become more proactive by noticing when a normal supplier or purchase disappears, but only after enough history exists to distinguish a meaningful change from ordinary variation.
- **Constitution principle supported:** Silence is a feature; evidence before insight; Business Brain understands seasonality and missing suppliers.
- **Estimated effort:** Large
- **Expected product impact:** Medium
- **Dependencies:** Reliable identity, frequency history, seasonality, and evidence presentation.
- **Recommended implementation order:** 16

## 17. Introduce evidence-based supplier and price comparisons

- **Why it matters:** Comparable purchasing evidence can help owners understand cost changes and alternatives without unsupported “best supplier” claims.
- **Constitution principle supported:** Evidence before insight; trust before capability; meaning before data.
- **Estimated effort:** Large
- **Expected product impact:** High
- **Dependencies:** Task 12; normalized units and packaging; sufficient comparable observations.
- **Recommended implementation order:** 17

## 18. Add recipe and ingredient intelligence only when it has real memory value

- **Why it matters:** Connecting ingredients to products and prices could make Barni more useful to restaurants, but an empty or disconnected recipe destination would dilute the core product.
- **Constitution principle supported:** No unfinished surfaces; the core loop; every component must justify its existence.
- **Estimated effort:** Large
- **Expected product impact:** Medium
- **Dependencies:** Reliable product identity, categories, units, and a validated restaurant-owner use case.
- **Recommended implementation order:** 18

## 19. Reflect genuine Business Memory growth

- **Why it matters:** Subtle evolution can make learning visible and rewarding when it represents real knowledge breadth and reliability rather than artificial engagement mechanics.
- **Constitution principle supported:** Barni evolves through real knowledge; no fake XP; Barni is not gamified.
- **Estimated effort:** Medium
- **Expected product impact:** Medium
- **Dependencies:** Agreed evidence-based maturity criteria and a stable Business Memory model.
- **Recommended implementation order:** 19

# P3 — Future Vision

## 20. Build a conversational business memory

- **Why it matters:** Owners should eventually be able to ask natural questions and receive concise answers grounded in their own purchasing evidence.
- **Constitution principle supported:** Barni is an AI business companion; Search is memory; evidence before insight.
- **Estimated effort:** Large
- **Expected product impact:** High
- **Dependencies:** Trustworthy knowledge graph, evidence links, language strategy, and conservative uncertainty handling.
- **Recommended implementation order:** 20

## 21. Provide predictive purchasing guidance

- **Why it matters:** With enough reliable history, Barni could help owners anticipate recurring needs and meaningful cost pressure before they become problems.
- **Constitution principle supported:** Business Brain understands behavior and seasonality; evidence before insight; silence is a feature.
- **Estimated effort:** Large
- **Expected product impact:** High
- **Dependencies:** Longitudinal data, validated patterns, clear uncertainty language, and proven safeguards against unsupported predictions.
- **Recommended implementation order:** 21

## 22. Support multi-location business understanding

- **Why it matters:** Growing restaurant groups may need Barni to distinguish local behavior while explaining shared suppliers, products, and costs across locations.
- **Constitution principle supported:** Barni stores connected business knowledge; trust before capability; one canonical source of truth.
- **Estimated effort:** Large
- **Expected product impact:** Medium
- **Dependencies:** A validated multi-location customer need, mature identity models, and clear location-level evidence.
- **Recommended implementation order:** 22

## 23. Enable trusted collaboration around Business Memory

- **Why it matters:** Owners, managers, and accountants may eventually need to review, confirm, and act on the same knowledge without losing accountability or context.
- **Constitution principle supported:** Trust before capability; action before complexity; Accountant prepares trustworthy output.
- **Estimated effort:** Large
- **Expected product impact:** Medium
- **Dependencies:** Stable roles and permissions, canonical operational states, and validated collaboration journeys.
- **Recommended implementation order:** 23

# The Next Single Implementation That Creates the Highest User Value

**Establish one truthful operational state across Home, Feed, Invoice Review, and Accountant.**

This is the highest-value next implementation because Barni cannot feel intelligent if it gives conflicting answers about what is pending, reviewed, approved, duplicated, or ready. A single shared truth restores trust, clarifies the next action, shortens the daily journey, and creates the reliable foundation required by every later insight, export, completion message, and Business Memory update.

- **Constitution principle supported:** Trust before capability.
- **Estimated effort:** Medium
- **Expected product impact:** High
- **Dependencies:** Product agreement on the meaning of each operational state.
- **Recommended implementation order:** First.
