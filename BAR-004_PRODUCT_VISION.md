# BAR-004: Barni Product Vision

## Purpose

This document defines the permanent product vision for Barni. It should guide product strategy, feature decisions, design, engineering, and communication.

## What Barni Is

Barni is a Business Memory.

Barni is not invoice software.

Barni is not accounting software.

Invoices are how Barni learns. Every invoice teaches Barni something about the restaurant: its suppliers, products, prices, purchasing patterns, costs, and operations.

Every upload makes Barni smarter.

Over time, Barni turns stored business information into useful memory, clear context, and better decisions.

## The Product Promise

Barni should feel like hiring an intelligent operations manager, not buying another piece of software.

Barni helps restaurant owners:

- Save time.
- Save money.
- Make better decisions.

Every meaningful feature should advance at least one of these outcomes.

## The Experience

Every screen should reduce stress.

Every interaction should increase confidence.

Barni should make the state of the business easier to understand, not add more information to manage. It should surface what matters, explain why it matters, and offer a practical next step when the stored data supports one.

The experience should feel:

- Calm.
- Capable.
- Trustworthy.
- Human.
- Clear.
- Proactive without being alarming.

## How Barni Creates Value

Barni builds knowledge through ordinary business activity:

1. The restaurant uploads an invoice.
2. Barni learns the stored facts.
3. Repeated facts become history.
4. History reveals supported changes and patterns.
5. Supported patterns become useful context and conservative recommendations.
6. The restaurant owner acts with greater speed and confidence.

Barni must never pretend to know more than the stored data supports. Trust is more valuable than novelty.

## Product Decision Principles

When deciding whether to build, change, or remove something, ask:

1. Does this strengthen Barni's Business Memory?
2. Does it save the restaurant owner time?
3. Does it help save money?
4. Does it improve confidence in a business decision?
5. Does it reduce stress or operational risk?
6. Is the result clear to a non-technical restaurant manager?
7. Is every conclusion supported by stored business data?

If the answer to these questions is no, the product probably does not need the feature.

## Long-Term Standard

Barni succeeds when restaurant owners feel that the product understands their business, remembers what matters, and quietly helps them run it better every day.

The goal is not to manage more invoices.

The goal is to build a business that remembers.
